## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Convert anything to anything using natural language.

Type what you mean — "10 km to miles", "72 F to C", "3 rai 4 ngan to square meters" — and AUC gives you the answer instantly. No dropdown menus, no picking categories first. Just type and convert.

**Local-first, offline-first**
Common conversions — length, area, weight, volume, temperature, speed, time, and data — are computed entirely on your device using a deterministic conversion engine. No internet connection needed, no waiting.

**Understands natural language, in more than one language**
AUC recognizes conversion requests in English and Thai out of the box, including local Thai units like ไร่ (rai), งาน (ngan), วา (wa), and เส้น (sen). When a request uses a language or phrasing the on-device parser doesn't recognize, AUC asks an AI service to interpret it — the AI only figures out what you meant; the actual math always happens on your device, the same deterministic way every time.

**Scientific and engineering units too**
Pressure (psi, kPa, bar, atm), energy (joule, BTU, kWh), power (watt, horsepower, BTU/h), and force (newton, pound-force) are all supported, with built-in dimensional safety — AUC won't pretend to convert between physically incompatible units (like energy and power) just because you asked.

**Regional units, handled honestly**
Some units genuinely depend on where you are — AUC will ask which region or standard applies rather than silently guessing a number that might be wrong.

**Privacy-conscious by design**
No account, no login, no server-side database of your conversions. Your history and favorites stay on your device. See AUC's full privacy policy for exactly what is and isn't sent anywhere.

**Simple, focused, and free**
Open the app, type, get your answer. History and favorites for quick access. Copy or share any result in one tap. A single small banner ad supports free use of the app — nothing louder, and never interrupting a conversion.

---

*AUC covers a growing set of unit categories and languages, and its natural-language understanding — like any AI-assisted feature — can occasionally misread an unusual phrasing. It does not yet cover every unit in every country, and the AI does not understand literally everything you could type. When AUC isn't sure, it will tell you rather than guess.*
