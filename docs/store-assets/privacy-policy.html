Overview

AUC — AI Universal Converter ("AUC", "the app") converts natural-language text ("10 km to miles", "3 ไร่ to square meters") into a numeric result. Most conversions are computed entirely on your device by a deterministic conversion engine, using no network connection at all.

When the on-device parser cannot understand a request — an unfamiliar language, an unrecognized or region-specific unit — the app asks an AI service to interpret the wording only. The AI never computes your result; a fixed, on-device calculation always does that. This policy explains exactly what leaves your device, to whom, and why.

§

Data on your device

Your conversion history and favorites are saved locally on your device using Android's standard app-private storage. AUC has no user accounts, no login, and no server-side database of your conversions — this data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the Share action below.

§

When AI assistance is used

If, and only if, the on-device parser cannot resolve your input, AUC sends the text you typed — nothing else, no device identifiers, no history, no location — over an encrypted HTTPS connection to AUC's own backend, which asks Google's Gemini API to identify the quantities, units, and target unit you meant. That structured answer (e.g. 10, kilometer → mile) is sent back and validated on your device before the on-device engine computes the final number.

Why this design The AI is a language interpreter, never a calculator: it never produces your numeric result, and the Gemini API key it requires never exists inside the app itself — only on AUC's backend server.

If a unit genuinely depends on a region AUC can't infer (for example, a "bigha", whose size varies by Indian state), the app will ask you to clarify rather than guess.

§

Advertising

AUC shows a single, non-intrusive banner ad via Google AdMob to support free use of the app. AdMob may use the Android Advertising ID and device/app signals to select and measure ads, per Google's own advertising and privacy policies. AUC does not build its own advertising profile of you, and does not show ads while an AI request is in progress or in a way that interrupts a conversion. If an ad fails to load — no network, no fill — the app continues to work normally; ads never block or affect conversion accuracy.

§

What we don't do

  • No account creation, login, or personal profile of any kind.
  • No server-side database storing your conversions or history.
  • No sale of personal data, to anyone, ever.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage permissions requested.
  • No Gemini API key or Cloudflare credential is ever embedded in the app — both live only on AUC's backend server as protected secrets.
§

Retention & deletion

History and favorites remain on your device until you delete them individually in-app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall AUC — at which point they are gone, since no copy exists elsewhere. Text sent during AI fallback is used only to produce that one response; AUC's backend does not persist a searchable log of user queries. Brief, temporary diagnostic logs (e.g. an error code and timestamp) may be visible to the developer through Cloudflare's real-time debugging tools when the AI service itself fails, purely to fix that failure — these are not a permanent record and are not linked to any user identity.

§

Security

All network traffic — to AUC's backend and onward to Gemini — is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). The Gemini API key is stored exclusively as an encrypted secret on AUC's Cloudflare Worker and is never shipped inside the app, logged, or returned in any response.

§

Children's privacy

AUC is a general-purpose unit-conversion utility, not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
